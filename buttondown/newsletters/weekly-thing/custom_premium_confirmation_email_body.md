<!-- buttondown-editor-mode: plaintext -->Thank you for becoming a Supporting Member of [Weekly Thing](https://weekly.thingelstad.com/). It means a lot.

A note on what this is and isn't. Your contribution goes 100% to the nonprofit I'm supporting this year — you aren't paying for the newsletter. Weekly Thing is free for everyone and always will be. You're using the membership as a way to support that nonprofit, and I'm grateful you chose to do it through here.

Becoming a Supporting Member also brings you into the friends-and-family circle around the newsletter. The issues you'll receive are the same ones everyone else gets, and the circle comes with a few thank-yous:

- [Thingy](https://thingy.thingelstad.com/) now answers your questions with Claude Opus, the strongest model, and gives you double the daily allowance.
- In [Release Notes](https://notes.yourversionnumber.com/), the journal that comes free with the Weekly Thing, you can add Claude's commentary to the books you make, up to ten a year.
- Release Notes also marks each year you give with a badge, and your birthday email says thanks.

If you're already signed in to Thingy or Release Notes, sign out and back in once to pick up the change.

You can [read more about the program](https://weekly.thingelstad.com/members/), including which nonprofit is being supported this year. You can also change your subscription or payment method [here]({{ manage_subscription_url }}) at any time.
