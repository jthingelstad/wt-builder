<!-- buttondown-editor-mode: plaintext -->Explore Minneapolis. Collect digital art. Discover the city like never before.

The [612 POAP Challenge](https://612.poapchallenge.com/) is a citywide scavenger hunt running through Labor Day – Monday, September 1, 2025. Across Minneapolis, 31 iconic locations await – each tied to a unique [POAP](https://poap.xyz) (short for Proof of Attendance Protocol), a digital token you can collect as a memento of your visit. It’s like geocaching, but with art, technology, and hometown pride.

Whether you’re a lifelong local or new to the Twin Cities, this is your invitation to experience Minneapolis in a fresh, fun, and meaningful way.
